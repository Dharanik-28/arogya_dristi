# arogya_dristi
AI-assisted oral cancer screening, telemedicine and patient-care platform for remote tribal communities, from village screening to specialist treatment. Synthetic data only. NOT FOR CLINICAL USE.


Aarogya Drishti is a secure, deployable digital health platform built for the AAROHAN Inter-Centre Hackathon 2026, organised by ITDA Paderu, Alluri Sitharama Raju District, Andhra Pradesh.

In remote tribal areas, early signs of oral cancer such as persistent ulcers, white or red patches and abnormal growths often go unexamined, and patients are frequently lost between the first screening and specialist treatment. Aarogya Drishti addresses this gap by connecting patients, village health workers, PHC Medical Officers, specialists, diagnostic facilities, patient navigators and district administrators in one continuous workflow.

What it does:

Village health workers register patients, record consent and capture guided oral photographs on a budget Android phone, even offline.
Image quality is checked automatically (blur, darkness, glare), and an advisory AI service gives a transparent, versioned, non-clinical output.
Medical Officers review each case independently of the AI, agree, disagree or defer, and sign the clinical decision.
Specialists consult over secure, time-limited video calls, and physical hospital appointments and referral slips are generated in Telugu and English.
Biopsy and pathology are tracked through the full lifecycle, and navigators follow up on missed visits until treatment linkage.
District administrators see aggregate programme indicators without access to identifiable patient records.

Built with responsibility:

The AI is a research demonstration, never a diagnosis. It cannot confirm or stage cancer, cannot reassure a patient, and cannot bypass clinician review.
The clinical workflow keeps working even if the AI service fails.
Role-based and patient-level access control, private image storage, consent gates and an append-only audit trail protect patient data.
Only synthetic patient data and licensed educational images are used.
